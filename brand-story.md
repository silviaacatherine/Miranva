# Brand Story

Miranva began as a student-led idea with a simple mission: to give old clothes a new purpose.

The concept focused on collecting unused garments and transforming them into customized tote bags. By doing so, Miranva aimed to reduce textile waste while creating products that were both useful and meaningful.

In addition to upcycled products, the project also explored custom tote bags for events, organizations, and personal use.

Although the venture did not continue long-term, it remains an important chapter in my entrepreneurial journey. Miranva taught valuable lessons about sustainability, product development, teamwork, and turning ideas into reality.

Every startup begins with a vision. Miranva was ours.
