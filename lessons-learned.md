# Lessons Learned from Miranva

## What Went Well

- We identified a real-world sustainability problem.
- We worked together as a team.
- We explored product design and customization.
- We took the initiative to build something from scratch.

## Challenges We Faced

- Balancing startup work with academics.
- Finding consistent customers.
- Managing production and logistics.
- Maintaining long-term momentum.

## Key Takeaways

1. Great ideas need validation.
2. Execution is harder than ideation.
3. Consistency matters more than motivation.
4. Team alignment is critical.
5. Failure is not the opposite of success; it is part of it.

## Reflection

Even though Miranva did not become a long-term business, it gave me firsthand experience in entrepreneurship, sustainability, and problem-solving. The lessons learned from this project continue to influence how I approach new ideas today.
