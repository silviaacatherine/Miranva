# Business Idea

## Problem

Millions of clothing items are discarded every year, contributing to textile waste and environmental pollution.

## Solution

Miranva proposed transforming old and unused clothing into customized tote bags and reusable products.

## Target Audience

- Environmentally conscious consumers
- Students
- Event organizers
- Small businesses
- Individuals seeking personalized products

## Product Offerings

### Upcycled Tote Bags
Customers could provide old clothing to be transformed into tote bags.

### Customized Tote Bags
Personalized designs for events, organizations, and personal use.

## Revenue Model

- Individual custom orders
- Bulk event orders
- College fest merchandise
- Corporate gifting

## Impact

The goal was to promote sustainability while creating practical products with sentimental and environmental value.

## Status

Archived startup concept and student entrepreneurship project.
