# Miranva ♻️👜

## About

Miranva was a student-led sustainability initiative focused on transforming unused clothing into functional and personalized tote bags.

The idea was simple: instead of letting old clothes go to waste, they could be repurposed into stylish tote bags with a story behind them.

## Vision

To reduce textile waste while encouraging sustainable and meaningful consumption.

## What We Planned

- Upcycled tote bags from old clothes
- Customized tote bags for events
- Sustainable gifting solutions
- Personalized keepsake products

## Why It Mattered

Miranva aimed to combine creativity, sustainability, and entrepreneurship into a project that could make a small but meaningful impact.

## Outcome

Although the project was eventually discontinued, it remains an important milestone in my journey as a student entrepreneur and designer.

## Lessons Learned

- Customer validation is essential
- Execution matters as much as the idea
- Teamwork and communication are crucial
- Every project teaches valuable lessons

## Legacy

Miranva represents creativity, sustainability, teamwork, and the courage to start.
